About the ViceTaped app

ViceTaped uses a small private tool, run on the owner's own PC, that edits clips and publishes them to the ViceTaped accounts through the platforms' official APIs (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram API). It is not a public service: no one else can sign in to it, and it does not collect anything from viewers or other users. See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
