Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: the ViceTaped app only posts our own clips to our own accounts. It collects no personal data from viewers or other users, and the sign-in tokens it uses stay on our own computer.

This policy explains how the ViceTaped app (the "App") and this website handle information. The App and website are run by the individual behind the ViceTaped channels ("we", "us"), based in the United Kingdom. Contact: rawlobbyclips@gmail.com.

1. Who the App is for

The App is a private tool used only by us, on our own PC, to publish our own videos to our own ViceTaped accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. No one else can sign in to it or connect their account.

2. What the App accesses

When we connect our own accounts, each platform gives the App permission to act for that one account through its official API:

  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API; scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish): our account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) so the App can show which account is connected, the creator settings TikTok requires before posting (such as allowed privacy options), and the ability to upload and publish our videos and check their status.
  • YouTube (YouTube Data API; scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl): our channel's name and ID, the ability to upload our videos, add a first comment to our own videos, and read view, like and comment counts for our own uploads.
  • Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login; permissions instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish and instagram_business_manage_comments): our professional account's ID, username and account type, our remaining publishing allowance, and the ability to publish our Reels and add a first comment to our own Reels. During sign-in, Instagram sends a one-time code to a relay page on this site, which passes it straight to the App on our computer and stores nothing. If Instagram can't take a video straight from our computer, the App may place it briefly in our own cloud storage so Instagram can fetch it, then deletes it.

The App does not read, collect or store data about other people: no viewers, followers, commenters or other users' profiles, messages or contacts.

3. Where it is stored

  • Access and refresh tokens are saved in a private folder on our own computer. They are not uploaded to any server we run, and they are never shared.
  • The App keeps a local record of what it posted (video IDs, links, post times and view counts) on the same computer.
  • We do not run a database or server that holds anyone's personal data.

4. How it is used and shared

The data above is used only to publish our videos and to show us how they perform. We do not sell, rent or share it, and we do not use it for advertising. It is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a TikTok token is only ever sent to TikTok).

5. Keeping and deleting it

Tokens are kept until we disconnect the account in the App, which deletes them from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time on each platform: TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions), Google/YouTube (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Instagram (Settings › Apps and websites).

6. Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using our YouTube channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

7. This website

This website has no sign-up, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

8. Children

The App is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because the App holds no data about other people, in practice there will be none, but you can always email us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Law and contact

This policy is governed by the law of England and Wales. Questions: rawlobbyclips@gmail.com.
